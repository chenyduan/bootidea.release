# Bootidea

**Turn ideas into action.**

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Your local-first AI desktop.

Bring AI into your everyday work, with your desktop as the starting point. Bootidea is built around a simple idea: help you move from what you have in mind to what you want to get done.

### Local first. AI at your side.

- **Start where you work.** A desktop experience centered on your local workflow.
- **Make AI part of the process.** Intelligent assistance woven into everyday work.
- **Keep your ideas moving.** Less friction between an idea and the next step.
